About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Wayne State University, advised by Prof. Dongxiao Zhu.

My research focuses on large language model safety, privacy, and unlearning, with an emphasis on membership inference attacks, memorization behavior, and robust mitigation strategies for trustworthy AI systems.
